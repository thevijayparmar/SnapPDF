# Privacy Policy — SnapPDF

SnapPDF does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All PDF
processing happens entirely on your device.

## In detail

- **No network access.** SnapPDF never makes an outbound connection of
  any kind — no update checks, no analytics pings, no crash reporting
  sent anywhere.
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for and nothing tied to
  your identity.
- **No telemetry.** Usage isn't measured, logged externally, or shared.
- **Your files stay on your machine.** Every PDF, image, or setting
  SnapPDF touches stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Local settings only.** SnapPDF stores your preferences in a
  `config.json` file next to the app itself (or in your local AppData
  folder as a fallback) — this file never leaves your device and is
  deleted along with the app on uninstall.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be reflected here in the
GitHub repository, with the change visible in the file's commit history.
